# Question index — closed questions (`qN` registry, closed half)

This is the **resolved half** of the canonical, milestone-neutral `qN` registry.
Every row here carries a `DONE` status token — it has been answered and the
resolution is recorded. Ids are **globally unique** with `question-registry-open.md`
and are never reused.

Governance and open questions live in `docs/notes/question-registry-open.md`
(link it for the shared registry discipline).
