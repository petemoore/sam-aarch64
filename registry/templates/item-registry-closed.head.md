# Item index — closed items (`iN` registry, closed half)

This is the **resolved half** of the canonical, milestone-neutral `iN` registry.
Every row here carries a `DONE` or `WONTFIX` status token — it has been completed
or deliberately declined. Ids are **globally unique** with `item-registry-open.md`
and are never reused.

Governance, open backlog, and the shared registry discipline live in
`docs/notes/item-registry-open.md` (link it).
